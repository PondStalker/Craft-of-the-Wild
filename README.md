# Craft of the Wild (playtest)

Play Minecraft in Hyrule: Breath of the Wild (through Savage Wind, a PC port) draws Hyrule around your Minecraft game.
You play Minecraft as usual; Zelda's world, its weather, its people and its monsters are there with you.

**Download:** the newest `Craft-of-the-Wild-<version>.zip` from [Releases](../../releases/latest).

## What you need
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 16 GB of RAM, a graphics card with Vulkan 1.3, about 25 GB free.
- Your own copy of Breath of the Wild for Switch, updated to 1.9.0 and dumped from your own console
  (a folder with `exefs` and `romfs`). No game files are here, and none will be given out.
- Minecraft: Java Edition (the Microsoft account that owns it).

## How to play
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (like Documents).
2. Double-click `1 - Setup.bat` once.
3. Double-click `2 - Play.bat`. The first time, Zelda asks for your game files and builds the game once (10-20
   minutes).

The README inside the zip explains the controls and commands.

## Updates
They install by themselves: `2 - Play.bat` checks for a new version each time it starts and puts it in place (usually
a few MB). Your saves, your Minecraft world and the game built from your files stay.

## Found a bug?
Message on Discord: what went wrong, what you were doing, and roughly when.
